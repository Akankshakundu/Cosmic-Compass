🌌 Milky Way

Our home galaxy

The Milky Way is the galaxy that contains our Solar System. It is a vast spiral galaxy filled with billions of stars.

🌠 Structure of the Milky Way

The Milky Way is a spiral galaxy with a central bulge and multiple spiral arms. It contains stars, planets, gas, and dust.

📍 Our Position

Our Solar System is located in one of the spiral arms called the Orion Arm, far from the center of the galaxy.

🌌 Size and Scale

  • Contains over 100 billion stars
  • Diameter: about 100,000 light-years
  • Takes light thousands of years to travel across

🕳️ Galactic Center

At the center of the Milky Way lies a supermassive black hole known as Sagittarius A*.
