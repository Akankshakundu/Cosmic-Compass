What Can You See Tonight?

This guide helps beginners observe the night sky easily.

🌙 Moon Phase

The Moon is one of the easiest objects to observe. Check its shape and brightness in the sky.

🪐 Visible Planets

Planets like Venus, Mars, and Jupiter are often visible without a telescope. Look for bright, steady lights.

⭐ Stargazing Tips

  • Find a dark place away from city lights
  • Let your eyes adjust to the dark
  • Use a sky app if needed
  • Be patient and observe carefully
