🌕 Venus

Twin of earth

Home | Stargazing | Learn Space

Venus is the second planet from the Sun, often called Earth’s “twin” because of its similar size and structure, but it is the hottest planet in the solar system with a toxic atmosphere and crushing surface pressure. It has no moons, rotates in the opposite direction of most planets, and shines as the third brightest object in Earth’s sky after the Sun and Moon.

📌 Key Facts

  • Distance from Sun: ~108 million km
  • Rotation day: 243 earth days
  • Orbital period(year): 225 earth days
  • Orbital speed: 35 km/s
  • Has water and atmosphere

🌡️ Unique features

It is the third brightest objectin the sky after the sun and the moon. It has no intrinsic magnetic field like earth. Instead, solar wind interacts directly with its atmosphere, creating an induced magnetosphere. It has no rings and moons. It spins clockwise, opposite to Earth and most planets.
