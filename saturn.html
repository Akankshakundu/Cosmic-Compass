🪐 Saturn

The Planet with rings

Home | Stargazing | Learn Space

Saturn is the sixth planet from the Sun, famous for its spectacular ring system. It is a gas giant, second only to Jupiter in size, with dozens of moons and a powerful magnetic field.

📌 Key Facts

  • Sixth planet from the Sun
  • Distance from Sun: 1.43 billion km
  • Rotation(Day): 10 hours,33 minutes
  • Orbital Period(Year): 29.5 Earth years
  • Orbital Speed: 9.7 km/s

🔴Unique Featuures

It has atleast 146 confirmed moons.Titan is the largest moon which is even bigger than Mercury. Most extensive and complex ring system in the solar system ,made of ice and rock particles. It's ring system is divided into seven main groups(A-G), spanning hundreds of thousands of kilometres. It's density is lowest of all planets(0.687 g/cm³), less than water- Saturn would float in giant bathtub. It's magnetic field creates auroras at the poles.
