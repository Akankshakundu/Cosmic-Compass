Our Solar System

The Solar System consists of the Sun and all the objects that orbit around it, including planets, moons, asteroids, and comets.

☀️ Sun

The Sun is the center of our Solar System and provides light and heat to all planets.

🪐 Mercury

Mercury is the closest planet to the Sun and has extreme temperatures.

🌕 Venus

Venus is the hottest planet due to its thick atmosphere.

🌍 Earth

Earth is the only planet known to support life.

🔴 Mars

Mars is known as the Red Planet and may have had water in the past.

🟠 Jupiter

Jupiter is the largest planet and has a giant storm called the Great Red Spot.

🪐 Saturn

Saturn is famous for its beautiful ring system.

🔵 Uranus

Uranus rotates on its side and has a bluish color.

🔷 Neptune

Neptune is the farthest planet and has very strong winds.
